# Code of Conduct

Chennai React welcomes everyone to our events, online spaces, and community with curiosity, friendliness, open-mindedness, and respect. All participants, speakers, sponsors, volunteers, and organisers are expected to follow our [full Code of Conduct](https://www.chennaireact.in/coc).

We are committed to a harassment-free experience regardless of gender, gender identity or expression, age, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, religion, or technology choices. Harassment and unwelcome sexual attention are not tolerated. Participants asked to stop inappropriate behaviour must do so immediately. Organisers may take appropriate action, including a warning or removal from an event.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation, contact an organiser or email [chennaireactjs@gmail.com](mailto:chennaireactjs@gmail.com). You may also contact the Code of Conduct team directly:
